What is Plogging!

-Plogging is the activity of picking up trash while jogging. It was popularized in sweden as being a way to remain active while cleaning up your enviorment. Plogging comes from the swedish word "Plogga" that means jogging in swedish. Plogging can be done doing any activity such as walking, running or hiking.

Why is it Important!

-Many people see litter in their comminuty as gross and not their problem leading to the trash to pile up. As a way to take agency people have began Plogging to provide a safe and clean enviorment for themselves and the all the other creatures that inhabit the space.

Our Goal!

-Through the creation of "this website" we want to facilitate communities to track their progress of trash collection with leaderboards. On a week by week basis, our organizations tab allows you to compare your trash collections and activity times scored against your friends and family! Be the change you want to see.
